How it works

Four steps, no account.

Step 01

Choose your amount

Take one of the limited packs or enter any custom amount from 10,000 Robux.

Step 02

Pick your coin

Bitcoin, Litecoin or Ethereum. The exact amount is reserved for you alone.

Step 03

Scan the QR or copy the address. We watch the chain and detect your transfer automatically.

Step 04

Get your Robux

Once confirmed, we deliver to the Roblox account you entered. You get an order reference to track it.

Questions

Good to know.

How fast is delivery?
Payments are detected automatically as soon as they are broadcast, but we only hand over Robux after the transfer has enough confirmations (1 for Bitcoin, Litecoin, Solana and USDT, 2 for Ethereum). After that, delivery is handled manually by our team and normally takes minutes — you can follow the status live on your order.
Why is the amount slightly odd?
Every order gets a unique amount, down to the last decimal. That is how several pending payments stay distinguishable on the same address, and how your transfer is matched to your order without you telling us a transaction ID. Send the exact amount shown — not more, not less.
What if I send the wrong amount?
We detect underpayments too (anything above 90% of the expected amount) and flag them for manual review. If that happens, open a Discord ticket with your order reference and we will sort it out. Never send a different amount on purpose to "top up" an order.
Do I need an account?
No. You only enter the Roblox username that should receive the Robux, plus your Discord in case we need to reach you. Keep your order reference — it is the only way to reopen the tracking page.
What happens if the price moves?
Your price and exact crypto amount are locked when you create the order and stay valid for 30 minutes. If the timer runs out, just create a new order — the rate is refreshed with the current market price.
Which coins can I use?
Bitcoin, Litecoin or Ethereum. For smaller orders Litecoin and Ethereum usually cost less in network fees than Bitcoin, so pick whichever is cheapest to send for your amount — the price you pay is the same in every coin.
