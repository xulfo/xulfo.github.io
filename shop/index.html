How plans work

From payment to key, in four steps.

Step 01

Pick your plan

Weekly, monthly or lifetime. Every plan unlocks the whole catalog — there is no free tier.

Step 02

Pay in crypto

Bitcoin, Litecoin or Ethereum. Your exact amount is reserved for you, so a payment can never be mixed up.

Step 03

Copy your key

The moment the transfer confirms we generate your key and show it on this page — ARC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX.

Step 04

Activate in Arc

Open the executor and paste the key into the popup. It binds itself to that PC — one key, one machine.

  • Hardware-bound. Your key locks onto the first PC that activates it, so a leaked key is useless to anyone else. Switched PCs? Ask on Discord and we unbind it for you.
  • Self-deleting keys. An expired or revoked key is removed from our system the moment it stops being valid — the executor drops it and asks for a new one.
  • No Roblox login needed. Plans are for the executor, so buying one never asks for your account details.
