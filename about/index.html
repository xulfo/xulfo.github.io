ABOUT ARC

Arc is a modern security research platform for authorized game penetration testing, exploit detection, and strengthening anti-cheat systems. It is built around three simple ideas: it should be reliable, it should be fast, and it should respect the people who use it.

What we do

We build and maintain a tightly optimized toolset for controlled testing, vulnerability analysis and validating defensive systems. It is delivered through signed licenses, hardware-bound access and a backend designed to stay online.

Built for reliability

Subscription timers pause during downtime, updates go out quickly whenever they are needed, and every release is verified before it goes live. When something does break, our aim is to make it right without you having to think about it.

Security first

Your account is protected with hashed credentials and signed sessions, and every license is cryptographically verified. We collect only what we genuinely need and never sell your data — the full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Community

A lot of what we build is shaped by the people who actually use it. The fastest way to reach us, get support or suggest an idea is through our Discord.

Stay safe from scams

get-oxide.com is the only official domain for Arc. Any other site claiming to offer it is unauthorized and may be handing out malware, so always check you are on get-oxide.com before you download anything or enter your details.

A valid license is always required. Anyone advertising a "free", "no license" or "cracked" version is trying to scam you, and those builds often hide keyloggers, stealers or other malicious code. Official downloads only come through the site, behind a login and an active license.

A few common scams worth avoiding:

  • Sites promising "free Arc" or a version with "no license".
  • Discord or Telegram groups selling "cracked" copies.
  • Look-alike domains pretending to be the official website.
  • "Key" or "license" generators.
  • Resellers that are not connected to get-oxide.com.

If something looks off, verify it with us on our official Discord before you trust it.
